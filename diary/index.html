Privacy & Storage

Location Tracking & Audio Retention

Disable Location & Weather

Stop tracking GPS and weather context when saving new entries.

Audio Retention Policy

Automatically delete heavy raw audio recordings after a certain period to save disk space. Transcribed text is kept forever.

Save Audio Externally

Instead of hiding audio in the app's internal database, save raw `.webm` audio files to the public Music/AuraDiary folder on your phone's main storage so you can easily access them.

v1.2.0-web2apk

  • Settings Reorganization: Redesigned the Settings page with dedicated categories and logical sub-pages for improved organization.
  • Navigation Adjustments: Accessing Main Settings no longer hides the sidebar, keeping navigation seamless. Sub-menus now focus completely on their content.

  • Native Android Conversion: Successfully migrated the web-based diary into a standalone Android APK using WebViews.
  • Settings Navigation: Added a sub-page back button to Settings for smoother navigation.
  • Hidden Sidebar Mode: The main sidebar is now hidden automatically when entering deeper level menus like Settings or Changelogs to provide more real-estate.
